This is a descriptive contrast, not a significance test. A single shuffle is not a confidence interval. Weak signed-return ACF does not mean returns are independent. Positive magnitude ACF does not prove long memory, predict direction or establish a trading edge.

What is measured

For each transformation, subtract its full-window mean. At lag k, divide the sum of paired centered products by the sum of all centered squares. This is the standard biased sample ACF, not a rolling regression or pairwise Pearson estimator. Lags count observed sessions, not calendar days.

The shuffled sequence is a deterministic Fisher-Yates permutation (seed 991). It preserves every return and its empirical distribution, but not the original order. The orange RMS line is a trailing 20-return root mean square, not a forecast or annualized standard deviation. It starts only after 20 observations.

Where the data stops

Split- and dividend-adjusted daily closes from the archived Quandl WIKI dataset. These are old observations, not a live feed. AAPL and MSFT are two selected surviving technology stocks, not a diversified market sample.

Finite samples, heavy tails and changes in volatility regimes can affect ACF estimates. A 60-lag picture cannot establish asymptotic long-range dependence. Window selection is exploratory and can change the result. The synthetic control demonstrates a specified model, not an estimated market process.
